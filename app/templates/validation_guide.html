NSF Compliance Validation

Automatic validation for banned substances and NSF certification requirements

What is Automatic Validation?

Every product you add or update is automatically checked for:

  • Banned Substances - Ingredients are screened against 290+ banned substances for NSF Certified for Sport
  • NSF Requirements - Ensures all required fields for certification are completed
  • Data Quality - Checks for missing or incomplete information
  • High-Risk Patterns - Flags products with names/descriptions that often contain banned substances
Validation happens automatically! Every time you create or update a product, the system checks for compliance issues.
Banned Substances Check (290+ Substances)

Based on the WADA Prohibited List, the system checks for:

Major Categories:
  • Anabolic Agents (Steroids & SARMs)
  • Peptide Hormones (EPO, HGH, etc.)
  • Beta-2 Agonists
  • Hormone Modulators
  • Diuretics & Masking Agents
Additional Categories:
  • Stimulants (DMAA, ephedrine, etc.)
  • Narcotics
  • Cannabinoids (THC, CBD)
  • Glucocorticoids
  • Gene & Cell Doping
Critical: If a banned substance is detected, the product is NOT NSF certification-ready and must be reformulated.
NSF Requirements Check

The system verifies that products have all required information for NSF certification:

Required Fields:
Product Information:
  • SKU Code
  • Product Name
  • Brand Name
  • Product Type
  • Net Content
  • Serving Size
Manufacturing:
  • Manufacturer Name
  • Facility Location
  • City, State, Country
  • Complete Ingredient List
  • Ingredient Amounts & Sources

Warning Levels
CRITICAL

Banned substances detected or major compliance issues. Product is NOT NSF-ready.

WARNING

Missing required fields or data quality issues. Address before NSF submission.

INFO

Suggestions for improving data quality. Product can proceed to certification.
