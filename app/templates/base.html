SKU Database Tracker - Help
What is this tool?

This tool helps you manage product SKU data and prepare it for NSF certification submissions.

NSF Certification Types
  • NSF Certified for Sport: For dietary supplements used by athletes (tests 290+ banned substances)
  • NSF/ANSI 173: Dietary supplement certification
  • NSF 229: Dietary supplement ingredient certification
  • NSF 527: Vitamin and mineral supplement certification
Exporting Data

You can export your SKU data in Excel, PDF, or CSV formats to share with:

  • NSF International for certification
  • Manufacturing partners
  • Suppliers and vendors
  • Quality control teams
