NSF Certification Overview
NSF Certified for Sport Requirements:
  • Testing for 290+ banned substances
  • Compliance with NSF/ANSI 173 standards
  • Complete ingredient disclosure
  • Facility audits and quality control
  • Annual re-testing and certification renewal
Required Documentation:
  • Complete formulation with ingredient amounts
  • Manufacturing facility information
  • Label claims and intended use
  • Safety Data Sheets (SDS)
  • Certificate of Analysis (COA)
  • Quality control procedures
