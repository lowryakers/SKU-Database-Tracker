NSF Certification Guide
Required Information for NSF Certification:
  • Product Information: Name, SKU code, brand, category
  • Formulation: Complete ingredient list with amounts
  • Manufacturing: Facility details, location, batch/lot numbers
  • Supplier Data: Contact information and addresses
  • Label Claims: All product claims and intended use
  • Documentation: SDS, COA, label images
  • Quality Control: Testing results and procedures
  • Contact Info: Primary contact for certification
Tip: For NSF Certified for Sport, products must be tested for 290+ banned substances and meet NSF/ANSI 173 standards.
